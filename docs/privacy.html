Privacy policy

Plain language on what Luna reads, what stays on your devices, and what touches the server when you link them.

On this page The short version PC & browser On your phone Storage & sync Counts & feedback Deleting your data

The short version

  • Only what's in front. Luna checks which app or site is active so it can block it. It never looks inside your apps, chats, or files.
  • Local first. Everything stays on your devices unless you link them. Linked devices sync through Luna's server.
  • No surveillance or ads. No screenshots, no keystrokes, no ads, and no third-party analytics.

PC & browser

On your PC

Every 5 seconds during a session, Luna checks which program or site is in front and how long since you last used the mouse or keyboard. It stores spans of study time, not individual checks.

Window & page titles are read only when you turn on the AI judge for a session. A local model on your own PC reads the title — it is never stored or sent off your machine.

In your browser

The extension tells Luna on the same PC which site is in the active tab, its page title, and whether it is playing sound. It sends this only over a local connection that never leaves your computer.

On your phone

Luna asks for each permission on its own screen and explains what it reads, does, and keeps first.

Usage accessSees which app is in front so Luna knows when you leave your allowlist.
Display over other appsShows the block screen, Nudge card, or Away cover over a blocked app. Reads nothing.
NotificationsShows a countdown notification so Android keeps the session lock running.
Notification accessHolds notifications during work rounds and returns them at your break. Held notifications (app, title, text, time; at most 200) stay on the phone and are never synced.
Do Not Disturb accessQuiets the phone during work rounds while allowing calls and texts from your Android starred contacts. Luna never reads your contacts.
Accessibility (Guard level only)Sends you Home from blocked apps, reads the browser's address bar to block sites, and blocks the Settings pages that would switch Luna off.
Device admin (Guard level only)Stops Luna being uninstalled during a session. It reads nothing.

Storage & device sync

What Luna keeps: Your lists, builds, schedule, sessions, and blocked tries in each session. After 90 days, a session's blocked tries are replaced by its totals so History can still show your weeks.

When you link devices: Linked devices sync through Luna's server, hosted on Render. Each device gets its own token stored only as a hash on the server, and you can remove a device at any time from Link devices. The server deletes blocked tries 120 days after they happened, once totals are saved.

Held notifications, window/page titles, and AI judge verdicts are never synced.

What Luna counts & feedback

Anonymous install ping: Once a week, the PC and phone apps send a random number made locally, "Windows" or "Android", and the app version so Luna can measure active installs. It is not linked to an account, name, sessions, or lists, and no IP address is stored. Turn it off any time with Count this install in Settings.

Luna Plus button: Tapping I'm interested on the homepage adds one to an anonymous tally, with no cookie or personal data.

Bug reports: If you submit a report via the bug report form or email, we only use what you send to diagnose and reply to your report.

Deleting your data & questions

Uninstalling Luna deletes everything kept on that device. To delete what the server holds, unlink every device, then email yzenyux@gmail.com.

Questions? Write to yzenyux@gmail.com. When this policy changes, the date at the top updates and the new version is posted here.
