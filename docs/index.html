Set up in three steps

Each part works on its own. Install the ones you need; link them and one session locks them all. Because Luna is in direct beta, each device asks you to approve a setting once.

1

Install Luna on your PC

The tray app runs your sessions and blocks programs. Start here.

Download for Windows

Windows warning: The beta installer isn't signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

2

Add the browser extension

Blocks sites in Chrome, Edge and Brave. Then press Connect browser in Luna on your PC.

Download extension (.zip)

Manual beta load: Unzip it, open chrome://extensions (or Edge/Brave), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and enable Allow in Incognito.

3

Put Luna on your phone

Scan with your phone's camera to download the Android app.

Download for Android

Android settings: Allow browser installs. On Android 13+, open App info › ⋮ › Allow restricted settings so Guard and Notification access can turn on.

To link it, open Luna on your PC › Link devices and scan the code shown there.

How a session works

Pick what pulls you away

Put apps, programs and sites on your lists once. Luna remembers them on every device you link.

Start a session

Choose how long and how strict. Until the time is up, what's on your lists stays closed, on every linked device.

Make it a habit

Set times in the Schedule and Luna asks when it's time. History shows how your weeks went.

What Luna reads, and where it stays

To block something, Luna has to know it's open. Here is everything it looks at.

Read the privacy policy
  • Which app or site is in front. On Android this needs Usage access and Accessibility; Luna asks for each and says why.
  • Page titles, only if you turn on the AI judge. A model on your own PC reads them. Nothing goes to an AI service.
  • Your lists, sessions and history stay on your devices. Linking syncs them through Luna's server, hosted on Render.
  • No ads and no tracking.

Help

Step-by-step pictures for every part are in the setup guide.

Windows says it protected my PC

The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

My phone won't install the app

Luna isn't on Google Play yet. When your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps (and tap Install anyway if Play Protect warns), then open the downloaded file again.

Android says "Restricted setting" for Accessibility or Notifications

Android 13 and later blocks Accessibility and Notification access for apps installed outside Google Play until you unlock them once. Open App info for Luna, tap ⋮ › Allow restricted settings, then return to Luna setup.

How do I load the beta browser extension?

Download and unzip Luna-Extension.zip, open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions / brave://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, pick the unzipped folder, and enable Allow in Incognito. Then press Connect browser in Luna on your PC.

Do I need all three?

No. The PC app and the phone app each work alone. The browser extension needs the PC app. Link them so one session locks everything.

How do I link my phone and PC?

On your PC open Luna › Settings › Link devices. On your phone tap Scan QR code and point it at the code.
